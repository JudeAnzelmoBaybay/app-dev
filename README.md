## 1. Movie Synopsis

*I Want to Eat Your Pancreas* is a Japanese animated drama movie released in **2018**. It follows a quiet high school boy who discovers the secret diary of his classmate, **Sakura Yamauchi**. Through her diary, he learns that she has a terminal illness affecting her pancreas.

Although they have very different personalities, they begin spending time together and sharing new experiences. As their friendship grows, Sakura teaches him to appreciate life, connect with other people, and value the time he has with them.

> "Life is precious, and every moment we share with others matters."

## 3. Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it has a **meaningful story** and *beautiful animation*. It shows how an unexpected friendship can change someone's life.

Some things I like about the movie are:

- The emotional story and memorable characters.
- The importance of friendship and kindness.
- The lesson about appreciating life.
- The way the characters change throughout the story.

## 4. Important Lessons

1. Appreciate the people around you.
2. Make the most of the time you have.
3. Do not judge people based on their personalities.
4. Small moments can create lasting memories.

## 5. My Favorite Character

My favorite character is **Sakura Yamauchi** because she is cheerful, kind, and full of life despite her illness. She encourages others to enjoy life and build meaningful connections.

## 6. My Review

I would recommend this movie to people who enjoy emotional stories about friendship and life. It reminds us that the time we spend with others can have a lasting impact.
